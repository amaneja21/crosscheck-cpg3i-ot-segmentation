# Engineering note — Filtration turbidity analyzer placement

The turbidity analyzer added to the Filtration area (asset 10.13.0.12) is installed
inside the same hazardous-area electrical classification boundary as PLC-2, under
the facility's existing Class I Division 1 equivalent rating for that room.
Introducing a second managed network device inside that boundary would itself
require a new intrinsically-safe barrier and re-certification of the enclosure,
which is not planned for this fiscal year and is not required for the analyzer's
function. The analyzer and PLC-2 share intrinsically-safe wiring and the same
physical segment by necessity of the electrical classification, not by omission.

Filed by: Filtration area electrical engineer of record.
